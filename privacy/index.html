Privacy

Updated 2026-09-30

Hangul Pace is a typing test that runs in your browser. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no ads, and what you type never leaves your browser.

Analytics, only if you say yes

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and finished tests. It loads only after you choose Accept in the Analytics row at the bottom of the page. If you choose Decline, or choose nothing, the Google tag is never loaded and nothing is sent. You can change your answer at any time with Analytics settings under How it works.

When you accept, Google Analytics receives page views and these events:

  • test_start and test_complete, with the test mode, speed (WPM and 타 a minute), accuracy, whether the keymap and hints were on, and whether the test came from a challenge link
  • share_open, share and share_fail, with the test mode, your WPM and how you shared (copy, download, an app), never the link or the picture
  • challenge_land and challenge_complete, with the test mode, the WPM to beat, your WPM and how the link was shared, never the words
  • restart, practice_start, keymap_toggle, hints_toggle, how_it_works_open and consent_decision
  • ime_detected, at most once per page load, with only the browser family and operating system family, when a Korean input method on your computer interferes with the test (so we can fix it)

Google also records the usual technical details of a visit, such as the browser, device type and an approximate country derived from a masked IP address, and sets its _ga cookies to recognize a returning browser. The text you type is never sent. We do not sell or share anything, and nothing is used for advertising. Google keeps event data for 14 months.

Sharing a result

Share pictures are drawn in your browser and never uploaded. A challenge link holds only a number that picks the words, the test length, your WPM and the word list version: no name and no ID. When you open a shared link, how it was shared (a copied link, WhatsApp and so on) is kept in that tab's storage, under the prefix below, until it goes out once with a page view, which happens only if you accept analytics. Google Analytics never receives the link itself.

What stays in your browser

Your settings (keymap and hints), your last and best speed for each test length, whether you have finished a test before, your analytics choice, and how many first-time tips you have seen are kept in your browser storage under keys starting with hangul-typing.v1.. They never leave your device. Clearing this site's data removes them.
